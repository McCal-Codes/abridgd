Typeface

Headlines got a personality

Every headline in the app now sets in Fraunces instead of quietly falling back to the system font. Bold means bold, too.

Fixed

"Next" now means next

Onboarding could get stuck on slide one no matter how many times you tapped Next. It scrolls all the way through now.

Fixed

No more déjà vu crash

A rare loop between profile and saved-articles state could freeze the app on first run. Found it, killed it.
